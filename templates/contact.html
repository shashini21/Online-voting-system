❓Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register as a voter?

Click on the "Register" button in the navigation menu, select "Voter" and fill out the registration form with your details.

How can I submit a nomination?

Register as a nominee, log in to your dashboard, and click "Submit Nomination" to enter your details for active events.

When can I view voting results?

Results are available immediately after the voting deadline closes. You can view them from your dashboard or the event page.
